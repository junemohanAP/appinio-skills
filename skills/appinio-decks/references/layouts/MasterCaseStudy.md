# MasterCaseStudy

Case study: headline and facts on white, challenge / approach / impact and quote on Blue 10.

Use for one-page client case studies (template p. 123).

**Provide:** client logo, headline outcome, industry / geography / method, challenge, approach, one impact figure, an approved quote. Never invent results or quotes — leave the bracketed placeholders until the client has approved them.

- Left column white (x 67–544): logo, 40px Bold headline, facts as label (`grey-300` SemiBold) over value (Bold).
- Right x 619–1920 on Blue 10: two columns of 551px — Challenge and Approach on top, Impact (61px ExtraBold blue figure) and the quote below.

**Master-template conventions** (all `Master*` components): title `slide-title` 45px ExtraBold ink with an optional 45px ExtraBold `grey-300` subtitle directly under it (delete it rather than leave a placeholder) — no eyebrow; footer = dark logo bottom-left at x 67, y 983 (144 × 38) with an optional 24px `grey-500` note at x 685 on the same row — no divider line; panels are Blue 10 (#E5EBFF) with white cards on them. Section padding `67px 67px 140px`; content fills down to y 940.
