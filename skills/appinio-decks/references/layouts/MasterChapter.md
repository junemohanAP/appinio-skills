# MasterChapter

Chapter divider as in the master: light gradient, grey number and white title on one line at the top.

Chapter divider for master-style decks (template p. 15). Results decks use `SlideChapter` (large number, title and description at mid-height).

- Gradient `focus-glow.jpg` full-bleed (the same gradient for every chapter in a deck).
- Number in `chapter-number` #CCCCCC and title in white, both 107px Bold, on one line at y 46: number at x 67, title at x 232. White logo bottom-left. No description line.

**Master-template conventions** (all `Master*` components): title `slide-title` 45px ExtraBold ink with an optional 45px ExtraBold `grey-300` subtitle directly under it (delete it rather than leave a placeholder) — no eyebrow; footer = dark logo bottom-left at x 67, y 983 (144 × 38) with an optional 24px `grey-500` note at x 685 on the same row — no divider line; panels are Blue 10 (#E5EBFF) with white cards on them. Section padding `67px 67px 140px`; content fills down to y 940.
