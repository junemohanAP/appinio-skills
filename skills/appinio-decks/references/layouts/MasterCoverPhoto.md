# MasterCoverPhoto

Cover on a full-bleed photo: white logo top-left, 171px white title, subtitle.

Use for company, sales and event decks where a person or moment should open the story (template pp. 9–11). Results decks use `SlideCover` on a gradient.

**Provide:** title (max two lines), subtitle, one photo from the Imagery group — a dark or mid-tone photo with calm space on the left.

- Photo full-bleed, `object-fit:cover`, listed first. A left-to-right ink scrim (72% → 20%) keeps white text legible; this is the only slide type where text sits on a photo.
- White logo top-left 280 × 73; title 171px Bold white at x 67, y 424; subtitle 40px Medium white at y 880.

**Master-template conventions** (all `Master*` components): title `slide-title` 45px ExtraBold ink with an optional 45px ExtraBold `grey-300` subtitle directly under it (delete it rather than leave a placeholder) — no eyebrow; footer = dark logo bottom-left at x 67, y 983 (144 × 38) with an optional 24px `grey-500` note at x 685 on the same row — no divider line; panels are Blue 10 (#E5EBFF) with white cards on them. Section padding `67px 67px 140px`; content fills down to y 940.
