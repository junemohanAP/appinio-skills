# MasterLogoWall

Client logo wall: title and a 4 × 3 grid of client logos.

Use for social proof (template p. 122).

**Provide:** official client logo files from the GDrive logo folder. Show them in their original colours and proportions — never recolour, stretch or redraw a client logo; get approval before using a logo in external material.

- Title 67px ExtraBold. Logos centred in equal grid cells (4 × 3 or 5 × 3), no boxes around them in the final slide — the dashed outlines here are placeholders.

**Master-template conventions** (all `Master*` components): title `slide-title` 45px ExtraBold ink with an optional 45px ExtraBold `grey-300` subtitle directly under it (delete it rather than leave a placeholder) — no eyebrow; footer = dark logo bottom-left at x 67, y 983 (144 × 38) with an optional 24px `grey-500` note at x 685 on the same row — no divider line; panels are Blue 10 (#E5EBFF) with white cards on them. Section padding `67px 67px 140px`; content fills down to y 940.
