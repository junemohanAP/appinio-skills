# MasterTable

Pricing or offer table: Blue 10 header band, hairline rows.

Use for pricing, packages, service scopes and any non-research table (template pp. 45–48, 110–111). Research results tables use the results-deck rules (sequential heat fills).

- Header band on Blue 10, radius 16 at both ends, 32px Bold. Rows 28px (first column SemiBold ink, others Regular `grey-700`), 1px `line` between rows, rows share the remaining height.
- Highlight one package column by colouring its header `blue-100` with white text.

**Master-template conventions** (all `Master*` components): title `slide-title` 45px ExtraBold ink with an optional 45px ExtraBold `grey-300` subtitle directly under it (delete it rather than leave a placeholder) — no eyebrow; footer = dark logo bottom-left at x 67, y 983 (144 × 38) with an optional 24px `grey-500` note at x 685 on the same row — no divider line; panels are Blue 10 (#E5EBFF) with white cards on them. Section padding `67px 67px 140px`; content fills down to y 940.
