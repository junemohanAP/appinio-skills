# MasterTextImages

Title, subtitle and three bold-led points left; a three-photo collage right.

Use for capability, offer or context slides with pictures (template pp. 21–27). For one half-slide photo use `SlideImage`.

**Provide:** title, optional subtitle, 2–4 points (bold headline + one or two Regular sentences), three photos from the Imagery group.

- Text in the left 860px column; collage in the right column: one tall photo 460px wide plus two stacked, 32px gaps, radius 16, all filling the height to y 940.

**Master-template conventions** (all `Master*` components): title `slide-title` 45px ExtraBold ink with an optional 45px ExtraBold `grey-300` subtitle directly under it (delete it rather than leave a placeholder) — no eyebrow; footer = dark logo bottom-left at x 67, y 983 (144 × 38) with an optional 24px `grey-500` note at x 685 on the same row — no divider line; panels are Blue 10 (#E5EBFF) with white cards on them. Section padding `67px 67px 140px`; content fills down to y 940.
