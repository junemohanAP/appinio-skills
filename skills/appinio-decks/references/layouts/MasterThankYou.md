# MasterThankYou

Closing slide: "Thank you!" (or "Q&A") with one wide event photo.

Use as the last content slide before `SlideBackCover`, or as a Q&A divider (template pp. 131–132, 139).

- Title 75px Bold ("Thank you!", "Q&A"); one wide photo filling the content area, radius 32. For a gradient-only Q&A, use `MasterChapter` without a number.

**Master-template conventions** (all `Master*` components): title `slide-title` 45px ExtraBold ink with an optional 45px ExtraBold `grey-300` subtitle directly under it (delete it rather than leave a placeholder) — no eyebrow; footer = dark logo bottom-left at x 67, y 983 (144 × 38) with an optional 24px `grey-500` note at x 685 on the same row — no divider line; panels are Blue 10 (#E5EBFF) with white cards on them. Section padding `67px 67px 140px`; content fills down to y 940.
