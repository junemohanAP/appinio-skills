# SlideAgenda

Contents: two columns of numbered chapters with page numbers and dividers.

Use once after the cover for decks with three or more chapters (the approved contents layout).

**Provide:** chapter numbers, titles (max ~28 characters) and page numbers. Up to three chapters per column, two columns; more than six: shorten to the main chapters.

- Title "Contents" in `slide-title-lg` (Inter ExtraBold 61px).
- Each row: number in Appinio Blue (Inter ExtraBold 45px), title Inter Medium 45px in ink, page number 40px in `grey-300`, right-aligned; a 1px `line` divider under every row, 40px padding above and below.
- An image variant (approved): three columns of 574 × 205 photos from the Imagery group, radius 16, each above the same number–title–page row.
- Footer with divider, note and logo as on every content slide.
