Use as the last slide of every deck.

- Gradient full-bleed (default `trendspotter.jpg`; any gradient from the group, but not the same as the chapters).
- Tagline verbatim in Inter ExtraBold 107px, white, top-left, on exactly two lines: "Rigorous, rapid research." / "Refreshingly human." (each `white-space:nowrap`). Never break inside "Rigorous, rapid research."
- Office addresses in the footer row from x 376, Inter 24px white, company names Bold. The template's Spanish address differs between its two back covers (Calle Víctor 5 vs Calle Bailén 1) — confirm which is current.
