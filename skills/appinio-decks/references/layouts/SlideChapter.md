# SlideChapter

Chapter divider on a gradient: large number, 107px title and one line of context, left at mid-height.

Use to open each chapter.

**Provide:** chapter number, chapter title (max ~24 characters), one line of context (max two lines), and one gradient from the Gradients group (default `focus-glow.jpg`; the same gradient for every chapter in a deck).

- One block, flush left at x 67, starting at y 300: the number at 200px Bold in white at 55% opacity, the title at 107px Bold white, the context line at 40px Regular white. This is the approved placement — never put number and title side by side at the top.
- Small white logo bottom-left (x 67, y 983, 144 × 38).
- Nothing else on the slide.
