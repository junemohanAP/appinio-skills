# SlideCoBrandCover

Cover for client or partner decks: both logos on one line, same height.

Use instead of `SlideCover` when a client or partner is named.

**Provide:** the partner's logo as a white (reversed) file, the title and the subtitle.

- Lockup top-left: Appinio white logo 280 × 73, a 2px white divider 73px tall, then the partner logo in a box of the same height (73px), 40px gaps. Neither logo is bigger; both sit on one centre line.
- Replace the dashed placeholder with `<img src=… style="width:280px;height:73px;object-fit:contain">`. Never ship the placeholder.
- On a white case-study slide the same lockup uses the dark Appinio logo and a 2px `#181D24` divider (approved template, case slide).
