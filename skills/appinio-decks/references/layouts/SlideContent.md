# SlideContent

Standard text slide: eyebrow, title, Regular body.

Use for any text slide.

**Provide:** a title (`slide-title` 45px with the optional `slide-eyebrow` above it, or `slide-title-lg` 61px for a message title, max two lines), body copy, an optional footer note.

- Body `slide-body`: Inter Regular 24px, line-height 1.4, `grey-700` (#39404C) — softer than the ink titles. Bold is for titles and numbers only.
- Text sits in the 860px left column; the right column holds a second text block, bullets or stays empty. For a picture use `SlideImage`.
- Footer: 1px `line` divider at y 900, the note under it at x 67, y 914 (24px, grey-500), the dark logo below at x 67, y 990 (144 × 38). Every content slide has the divider, even without a note.
