# SlideCover

Opens every deck: brand gradient, large white logo, 171px title.

Use as slide 1 of every deck without a partner.

**Provide:** a title of max two short lines, one subtitle line (market · fieldwork · n =) or none, and one gradient from the Gradients group (default `mindshare.jpg`).

- The gradient is a full-bleed `<img>` and the FIRST child of the section, so text paints over it.
- White logo top-left at x 67, y 68, 280 × 73. This is the largest the logo appears.
- Title `slide-cover-title`: Inter Bold 171px, line-height 0.96, white, at x 67, y 296. Subtitle Inter Medium 40px, white.
- Nothing else on the cover. Award or membership badges (ESOMAR, MRS) go bottom-right only when supplied as files.
