# SlideFactCards

Facts in Grey 10 cards with icons, filling the area — method, sample, limitations.

Use for methodology, sample facts, study limitations or any set of 4–6 parallel facts (approved card layout).

**Provide:** per card an icon from Icons Blue, a short label, a value (or none) and an optional line of detail.

- Grid of Grey 10 (#F7F7F7) cards, radius 16, padding 40, 32px apart, three columns of 574px (two of 877px for four cards). Rows are `1fr` so the cards fill the space down to the footer divider — no empty band under the content.
- Icon in a 72px Blue 10 tile (radius 12), label `slide-eyebrow` (24px Bold, uppercase, 2px tracking, `grey-700`), value Inter ExtraBold 45px, detail Regular 24px in `grey-700`.
