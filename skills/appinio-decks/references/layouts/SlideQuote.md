# SlideQuote

Portrait left, 72px quote right, attribution in grey.

Use for client testimonials and respondent verbatims.

**Provide:** the quote (max ~20 words at this size), the speaker's real name, role and company, and their approved photo. Never invent a quote or a person.

- Photo fills the left half (0–927 × full height), square edges.
- Quote and attribution sit in one pinned block at x 993, y 67, 860 × 953 (section padding can't exceed 256px). Quote Inter Bold 72px, in curly quotation marks. Attribution Inter SemiBold 32px in grey-500, bottom of the right column.
- For a verbatim without a photo, use `SlideStatement` styling at 72px on white.
