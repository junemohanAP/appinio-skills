# SlideSectionIntro

Chapter intro: eyebrow and title left, intro text right.

Use after a chapter divider when the chapter needs framing.

**Provide:** title, optional subtitle, 1–3 sentences of intro.

- Two columns of 860px with a 66px gutter (left x 67–927, right x 993–1853).
- Dark grey `slide-eyebrow` above the title (24px Bold, uppercase, 2px tracking), title `slide-title` (Bold 45px, ink).
- Intro text `slide-lead` (Inter SemiBold 32px).
