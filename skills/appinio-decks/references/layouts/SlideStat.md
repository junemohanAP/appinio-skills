# SlideStat

Big numbers: up to three 133px figures with label and meaning.

Use when one to three figures are the message.

**Provide:** per number a short label, the figure and one line on what it means.

- Three Grey 10 cards (574px, 32px apart, radius 16) that fill the space down to the footer. Label `slide-eyebrow` (Bold 24px, uppercase, dark grey `grey-700`), number `slide-big-number` (Bold 133px, ink), meaning `slide-lead` (SemiBold 32px).
- These columns are centred, as in the approved template; everything else on slides is flush left.
- The figure may be `highlight` blue (#0038FF) when one of the three is the story; never all three.
